Regular Expression Denial of Service

Send a crafted near-match to a target validator and measure the real cost of a vulnerable pattern versus its fix.

OWASP InjectionCWE CWE-1333LEVEL Medium–Hard
01 What it is

Understand the weakness

ReDoS happens when a crafted input makes a regular expression take excessive time to process. Nested or overlapping repetition can force a backtracking engine to try many failed possibilities. Repeated slow matches can consume CPU and reduce application availability.

OWASP: InjectionCWE-1333

04 Why it matters
What can go wrong?
Repeated slow matches consume CPU and can delay other work. Cloudflare reported that excessive regular-expression backtracking contributed to a July 2, 2019 outage.


05 The fix
Use predictable matching
Avoid nested or overlapping repetition, cap input size, and prefer predictable matching for untrusted input.
